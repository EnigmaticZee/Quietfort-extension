Good to know

  • Pin Quietfort to the toolbar (the puzzle-piece menu) to see what it blocks on each site.
  • Right-click anything on a page and choose Hide this element (for good) or Remove until reload (just this visit).
  • Or describe it in the Quietfort panel: “newsletter pop-up”, “chat bubble”.
  • Page not working? Press Site broken? and Quietfort finds the rule that broke it.
